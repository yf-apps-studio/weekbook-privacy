Privacy Policy

WeekBook — More than plans

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WeekBook handles information when you use the application. WeekBook is developed and published by YF Apps Studio.

1. General

WeekBook is a weekly planner and productivity application designed to help users manage tasks, reminders, lists and personal planning.

WeekBook does not require users to create an account or sign in to use the application.

2. Information stored in the app

Information that you enter into WeekBook, such as tasks, notes, lists, reminder settings and other planning information, is primarily stored locally on your device.

WeekBook does not operate its own online account system or server for storing your personal planner content.

3. Photos and files

If you choose to add a photo or file to WeekBook, the application accesses only the content that you select or authorize.

WeekBook may also allow you to export or import backup files. These actions are initiated by you, and you decide where exported files are stored or shared.

4. Microphone and voice input

WeekBook may request access to the microphone when you choose to use voice input or speech recognition features.

Microphone access is used only when you activate the relevant feature. Speech recognition may be processed by speech recognition services available on your device. Such services may be subject to the privacy policies of your device or service provider.

5. Notifications and reminders

WeekBook may request permission to send notifications so that it can provide reminders you create in the application.

You can manage notification permissions at any time through your device settings.

6. PIN and biometric authentication

WeekBook may provide optional PIN or biometric protection for access to the application.

Biometric authentication is handled by the operating system. WeekBook does not receive or store your fingerprint, face scan or other raw biometric information.

7. Data sharing

WeekBook does not sell your personal information.

WeekBook does not intentionally share your planner content with advertisers or data brokers.

Certain optional functions may use services provided by Android, your device manufacturer, or other system providers. Information processed by those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Advertising

WeekBook currently does not display third-party advertising.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because planner data is primarily stored locally on your device, you can delete individual content directly in WeekBook.

Removing application data or uninstalling WeekBook may delete locally stored information unless you have previously created a backup.

Backup files that you export remain under your control and must be deleted separately from the location where you stored them.

10. Children's privacy

WeekBook is a general productivity application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to design WeekBook so that personal planning information remains under the user's control. However, no device or digital storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WeekBook features or legal requirements change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
